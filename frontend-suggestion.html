Selected Disease: Tomato Early Blight

🌿 Organic & Natural Treatment

Prune off infected lower leaves immediately. Apply organic copper-based fungicide or neem oil solution every 7 to 10 days.

🧪 Chemical Remedy

Spray Chlorothalonil or Mancozeb fungicide at the first sign of leaf spots following package safety guidelines.

🛡️ Preventive Care

Rotate tomato crops every 2-3 years. Mulch soil surface to prevent fungal spores from splashing onto lower leaves.

💧 Watering & Soil Tips

Avoid overhead watering; use drip irrigation to keep foliage completely dry during early morning hours.
